Research

My research interests lie in transcendental number theory and Diophantine approximation, particularly in questions concerning the arithmetic nature of real numbers and methods used to establish transcendence results.

Transcendence and Diophantine Approximation

The first part of my master’s thesis studies foundational properties of Liouville numbers, which provide classical examples of transcendental numbers characterized by strong rational approximation.

Through this study, I aim to develop a clearer understanding of irrationality measures, approximation exponents, and the role of quantitative estimates in transcendence theory.

  • Liouville numbers and irrationality measures
  • Diophantine approximation
  • Foundational aspects of transcendence theory

Ongoing Direction

I am currently beginning to explore aspects of Baker’s theory, particularly linear forms in logarithms and their applications to transcendence and effective results in number theory. I am interested in understanding how analytic and algebraic techniques combine to yield explicit bounds and structural insights.

Broader Mathematical Themes

More broadly, I am motivated by conceptual and structural aspects of mathematics. I am especially interested in areas where analytic tools illuminate arithmetic phenomena, and in developing a rigorous understanding of abstract mathematical structures.
