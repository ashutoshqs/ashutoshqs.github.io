Research Interests

My research interests lie in analytic number theory, particularly modular forms, as well as in transcendental number theory and Diophantine approximation, with an emphasis on the arithmetic nature of real numbers and methods used to establish transcendence results.

Master’s Thesis (Ongoing)

I am currently working on my master’s thesis titled Transcendental Number Theory at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, under the supervision of Prof. Ekata Saha.

The thesis studies foundational approximation-theoretic properties of Liouville numbers, with emphasis on irrationality measures and structural aspects of classical transcendence theory. Through this work, I aim to develop a deeper understanding of quantitative methods in Diophantine approximation.

Analytic Number Theory

Alongside my work in transcendence theory, I am developing an interest in analytic number theory. I have studied foundational aspects of modular forms up to the theory of Hecke operators, and I am interested in how Hecke theory encodes arithmetic information through analytic structures. More broadly, I am drawn to Tauberian ideas and to analytic methods that reveal asymptotic behaviour of arithmetic functions.

Ongoing Direction

I am currently beginning to explore aspects of Baker’s theory, particularly linear forms in logarithms and their applications to transcendence and effective results in number theory. I am interested in understanding how analytic and algebraic techniques combine to yield explicit bounds and structural insights.

Broader Mathematical Themes

More generally, I am motivated by structural and foundational aspects of mathematics. I am especially interested in areas where analytic techniques illuminate arithmetic phenomena, and in developing a rigorous understanding of abstract mathematical structures that unify diverse problems in number theory.
