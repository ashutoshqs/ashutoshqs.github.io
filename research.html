Research

My primary interests lie in number theory, particularly in areas that connect arithmetic phenomena with analytic methods.

Number Theory

I am especially interested in transcendental number theory and Diophantine approximation. I am drawn to questions concerning the arithmetic nature of numbers and the analytic techniques used to study their structure.

I am also interested in analytic methods in number theory, and in understanding how tools from analysis illuminate deep problems in arithmetic.

  • Transcendental number theory
  • Diophantine approximation
  • Analytic methods in number theory

Broader Mathematical Themes

More broadly, I am motivated by foundational and conceptual aspects of mathematics. I am keen to develop a deeper understanding of abstract structures and the principles underlying them, particularly in areas that connect number theory with algebra and analysis.
